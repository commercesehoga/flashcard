Frequently Asked Questions

How does the image flashcard maker work?

Upload a JPG, PNG or WebP photo or screenshot of typed, handwritten or scanned notes. The text is read in your browser with OCR. Choose your exam and the number of cards, then generate flip flashcards with a question on the front and an answer on the back.

Which images give the best results?

Clear, well-lit images with English text work best. Very blurry, tiny or untidy handwriting may not be read correctly, so retake the photo or try a sharper scan.

Which exams are supported?

Class 12 CBSE, NEET, JEE, Banking, SSC, and a general "Other" mode for any other subject, exam, or certification.

Is my data stored anywhere?

Your image stays in your browser and is not uploaded. Only the extracted text is sent to the AI service to produce your flashcards, and a deck is kept in your browser only when you tap Save.
