Frequently Asked Questions

What are preset flashcard decks?

Preset decks are ready-made flashcard sets for common subjects and exam topics. Pick a topic and its deck opens instantly.

Do preset decks use my daily tokens?

No. Preset decks are already written, so they need no AI generation and no tokens.

How can I make a deck on my own topic?

Use the other pages of ThunderStudy AI to generate a deck from a topic, a PDF, a YouTube video, a webpage or an image.
